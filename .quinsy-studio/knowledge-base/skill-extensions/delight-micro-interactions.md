# Delight & micro-interactions (extension)

> Extends **UX-002**, FE-001. **Canonical rules:** [delight-playbook.md](../delight-playbook.md).  
> Subordinate to [anti-slop-design-ux.md](../anti-slop-design-ux.md). No new internal skill ID.

When scanning a flow for delight, **do not** inject joke loaders, wink icons, emoji chrome, or confetti on ordinary saves. Those fail anti-slop and Kano (excitement on unmet basics).

## Scan

1. Map empty, wait, first success, hard-work-done, recovery, buy/commit.
2. For each: are **basics** met (status, findability, honest price)? If no, fix craft — do not decorate.
3. Propose **at most one** moment per milestone, with reduced-motion.
