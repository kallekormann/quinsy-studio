# Team 11 — Growth Design

Owns **whether and how to test** commercial surfaces, and the conversion mechanics Team 10 must design to. Does not own visual system, IA home surface, or SEO.

Pairing: [design-team-canon-ledger.md](../../knowledge-base/design-team-canon-ledger.md) · [growth-mechanics-playbook.md](../../knowledge-base/growth-mechanics-playbook.md) · [delight-playbook.md](../../knowledge-base/delight-playbook.md) (peak–end on buy; no fake urgency).

## Director roles

| Skill | Role |
|-------|------|
| GD-001 | Growth Designer — hypothesis, metric, experiment, variant intent |
| GD-002 | Commercial surface mechanics — landing, pricing, checkout, PLP, PDP |

## Workflow

1. Commercial ticket (`marketing-page`, or checkout / PLP / PDP / pricing in title) → skills **append** `GD-001`, `GD-002` (Team 10 remains ticket `team`).
2. Team 11 fills spec **§6 Growth intent** (claim even when analytics are missing) including **peak–end**.
3. Team 10 designs the control (and a variant only if §6 names the one change).
4. Team 05 only for public URL SEO/AEO / volume copy.

Do not start an experiment when IA or the form is wrong — split an UX-003 / UX-002 ticket instead.
