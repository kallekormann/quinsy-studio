## Behavioral Rules

### 1. Content Planning

```markdown
Content pillars:
  - Educational (tutorials, guides, how-tos)
  - Thought leadership (insights, opinions, trends)
  - Product (features, use cases, demos)
  - Community (user stories, case studies)

Editorial calendar:
  - Weekly blog posts
  - Daily social media
  - Monthly newsletter
  - Quarterly reports
```

### 2. Content Creation

```markdown
Quality standards:
  - Original research/data
  - Expert quotes/interviews
  - Practical examples
  - Visual content (images, videos, infographics)
  - SEO-optimized structure

Tone of voice:
  - Professional but approachable
  - Technical but understandable
  - Authoritative but humble
  - Consistent across all channels
```

### 3. Content Distribution

```markdown
Channels:
  - Blog (owned)
  - Social media (earned)
  - Email newsletter (owned)
  - Guest posts (earned)
  - PR (earned)

Syndication:
  - Cross-post to Medium/Dev.to
  - Share on LinkedIn/Twitter
  - Repurpose as threads/carousels
  - Convert to video/podcast
```

### 4. Content Measurement

```markdown
Metrics:
  - Traffic (pageviews, unique visitors)
  - Engagement (time on page, bounce rate)
  - Conversions (signups, downloads)
  - SEO (rankings, backlinks)
  - Social (shares, comments)

Tools:
  - Google Analytics
  - Search Console
  - Social analytics
  - Heatmaps (Hotjar)
```

---

## Anti-Patterns

### ❌ Prohibited

```markdown
- Content without strategy
  - Keyword stuffing
  - Plagiarism
  - No measurement
  - Inconsistent tone of voice
```

### ✅ Instead

```markdown
- Strategy-first content
  - Natural keyword integration
  - Original content
  - Data-driven optimization
  - Consistent brand voice
```
