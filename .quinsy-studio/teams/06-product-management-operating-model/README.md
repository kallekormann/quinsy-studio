# Team 06 — Product Management & Operating Model

> Responsible for product management, user research, roadmapping and the operating model.

## Director-level roles

| Role | Level | Responsibilities |
|------|-------|------------------|
| **Product Manager** | Director | PRD analysis, roadmap, milestone downsizing, **staffing every ticket with the right teams and skills** |

The Product Manager staffs each ticket (PM-004). `./studio ticket` pre-fills skills and companions. `./studio groom` refuses the sprint while a ticket has no skills, an unknown ID, or lacks a required companion (security, privacy, accessibility, testing, payments, secrets, marketing email). During execute, the CARD adds a missing required companion and lists the skill files the implementer must read.
