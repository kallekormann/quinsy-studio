## Wedge and bundling

Propose folding the competition-pass rules into dimensions 7 and 9:

- Dimension 9 (competitor weakness): in a crowded or saturated space, an idea with no wedge cannot score above 2. A price-only wedge needs sourced evidence that the cost structure can stay lower.
- Dimension 7 (platform risk, regulation, and moat): name bundling risk, the chance a platform or incumbent ships the job as a free feature.
