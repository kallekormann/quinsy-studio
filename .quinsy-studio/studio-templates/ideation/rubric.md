# Rubric

Ten dimensions, each 0–5 in its own pass. Total is the sum of score times weight. Maximum 90. Empty is not 0 and not 3. Any empty dimension keeps the idea at `needs review`.

`validated` means all ten are filled, there is no knock-out, and the linked problem has at least three independent sourced lines. The shortlist top N is only ideas that are validated and total at least 58.

Independent means a different thread, a different author, and a different domain. Evidence needs a URL and a date, and must be from the last 24 months unless it is a legal duty that is in force.

1. Willingness to pay, weight 3. 0 nobody pays. 3 sparse paid workarounds, at least two sourced cases. 5 several sourced recurring payments with prices.
2. Demand volume, weight 2. State the proxy. 0 under about 1,000. 3 about 10,000 to 100,000. 5 above about 100,000.
3. Frequency and urgency, weight 2. 0 rare. 3 monthly or a deadline. 5 weekly or money or legal exposure.
4. Reachability, weight 3. 0 no channel. 3 one channel and a plausible cost. 5 they already look where a listing or search can find them, with a sourced cost.
5. Time to MVP and upkeep, weight 1. Builder time is not a ceiling. One solo builder, with AI agents for coding and design, ships in chunks of about two months. Each chunk is a full web app: big enough for that builder and those agents, and small enough to finish in about two months. The first chunk is the MVP. Later chunks stay in the idea. 0 the first chunk cannot be a web app in about two months, or the compliance is medicine or another clinical license. 3 the full solution needs later chunks, and the first chunk is a full web app inside about two months. 5 the whole solution fits in one such chunk.
6. Monetization, weight 1. Charge model only. 0 none. 3 model named, no comparables. 5 model plus at least three sourced neighboring prices.
7. Platform risk, regulation, and moat, weight 2. 0 blocked (also a knock-out). 3 host rules can change, no moat. 5 low dependence and some barrier to copying.
8. Trend, weight 1. 0 shrinking (knock-out unless a new legal duty). 3 flat, with evidence. 5 growing, with a sourced signal.
9. Competitor weakness, weight 2. Cite competitor files. 0 strong incumbent with no weakness (knock-out). 3 recurring sourced complaints. 5 expensive, outdated, or abandoned, plus an unserved non-customer.
10. Fit, weight 1. Sophistication is in reach. 0 medicine, clinical care, or hardware or a license this builder does not hold. 3 a non-clinical trade or life situation that needs domain learning. 5 an ordinary web app for a non-clinical trade.

Knock-outs: nobody paying, no channel, incumbent with no weakness, platform or legal block, medicine or clinical care, fewer than three independent sources, no linked problem, trend 0 unless a legal duty applies.

The wedge cap for crowded spaces is enforced by the competition pass until the proposal in rubric-proposals.md is approved into this file.
